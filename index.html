Distracted Driver Detection

Deep learning · computer vision

Classifies images of drivers into 10 behavior categories (safe driving, texting, talking on the phone, reaching behind and others) using ResNet50, built both from scratch and via transfer learning.

The transfer learning model (pretrained ResNet50, fine-tuned in two phases) was evaluated on the full validation set of 4,130 images from 5 held-out drivers.

Validation accuracy72.52%
Validation loss0.8653
Best validation accuracy during training74.67%
Final training accuracy98.02%

The ~23.5 point gap between training and validation accuracy indicates some overfitting. The model confuses visually similar classes, such as safe driving and adjusting hair or makeup.

Human Activity Detection

Computer vision · web app · in development

A web app that recognizes human actions in video by combining pose estimation with a sequence classifier. Detectron2 (keypoint_rcnn_R_50_FPN_3x) detects 17 body keypoints per frame, buffered in a sliding window of 32 frames. A custom-trained LSTM classifies the window into one of six actions: jumping, jumping jacks, boxing, waving with two hands, waving with one hand, and clapping.

The predicted label and skeleton overlay are drawn onto the output video. Progress streams to the browser through Server-Sent Events, and the annotated video can be viewed and downloaded.
